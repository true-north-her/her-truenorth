# TrueNorth Meet Our Team

## Goal
Add a reusable, accessible team-story section using the three supplied portraits and the existing TrueNorth design system.

## Build
- Create structured team data plus reusable `MeetOurTeam` and `TeamCard` components.
- Recreate the reference interaction: Komal expanded initially, with hover, focus, and click switching the expanded member.
- Use distinct Connector, Dreamer, and Builder card themes built only from TrueNorth colors and understated compass, horizon, celestial, botanical, and blueprint motifs.
- Add the editorial introduction and closing statement, placing the section before the Founding Circle.
- Keep portraits prominent and undistorted, with expanded-only descriptors, keywords, and identity labels.

## Responsive and Accessibility
- Use one expanding horizontal row on desktop and tablet, then a tap-to-expand vertical stack on mobile.
- Preserve readable names, visible faces, keyboard operation, clear focus, reduced-motion support, and no horizontal overflow.
- Verify interactions and presentation across desktop and mobile, plus browser and build errors.
