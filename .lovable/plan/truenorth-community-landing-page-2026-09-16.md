# TrueNorth Community Landing Page

## Goal
Create a polished, responsive landing page for TrueNorth as a calm, privacy-respecting community for young women. The page will use the supplied strategy as its source of truth and the reference website as visual inspiration, while shifting the message away from a dating-only journal toward the broader community vision.

## Page structure
1. **Header** — TrueNorth identity, restrained navigation, and a “Join the Founding Circle” action.
2. **Opening** — “A calmer way to live” as the central message, concise community positioning, and a prominent interest-form link.
3. **The shift** — contrast public performance and comparison with private reflection, honest conversation, and mutual support.
4. **Community pillars** — Reflection & Wellness, Girls Talking to Girls, and Mentorship.
5. **What happens here** — highlight a focused set of early activities such as Girl Circles, weekly questions, Skill Swaps, and “The Girl I Needed.”
6. **How TrueNorth grows** — explain the Community → Insight → Product approach without presenting unvalidated product ideas as launched offerings.
7. **Founding Circle invitation** — clearly frame the first 20–30 members as collaborators who help shape the community.
8. **Footer** — concise brand statement and relevant community links, without inventing contact details.

## Visual direction
- Preserve the reference site’s editorial, tactile feeling: generous whitespace, serif-led headlines, subtle paper details, soft motion, and an intimate rather than corporate tone.
- Follow the strategy palette: cream, deep navy/charcoal, dusty sage, and warm clay.
- Avoid wellness stock-photo clichés, glossy 3D illustrations, therapy-app pastels, excessive rounded cards, and generic marketing sections.
- Use original, community-oriented visual compositions rather than copying the dating-journal mockup literally.
- Keep mobile layouts clear and ensure the next section remains visible beneath the opening area.

## Content and interactions
- Write concise page copy from the strategy document in the voice of a perceptive, emotionally literate friend.
- Link every primary join action to the external interest form.
- Use lightweight, restrained transitions and respect reduced-motion settings.
- Add accessible navigation, descriptive labels, keyboard focus states, and meaningful image alternatives.
- Add unique page title, description, and social metadata for TrueNorth.

## Technical details
- Replace the current placeholder at the home page.
- Extend the existing semantic design tokens and load the selected editorial and humanist fonts correctly.
- Keep the page static; no account system or stored submissions are needed because applications go to an external form.
- Verify the finished page at desktop and mobile sizes, including the external-link behavior and visible layout.

## Needed before completion
- The public interest-form URL. Until supplied, the join actions will use a clearly isolated placeholder link that can be replaced without redesigning the page.
