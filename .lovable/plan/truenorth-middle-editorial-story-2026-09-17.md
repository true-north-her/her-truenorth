# TrueNorth Middle Editorial Story

## Goal
Turn the content between the video opening and footer into one slow, editorial narrative while preserving the existing opening, navigation, footer, routes, links, typography, and color system.

## Build
- Expand “The Shift” into a typography-led pause with generous space and restrained emphasis.
- Merge the community pillars and activity list into one magazine-style chapter containing four asymmetric experience moments.
- Add a directional bridge connecting community listening to what gets built.
- Replace the static growth blocks with an accessible compass-like loop for Community → Insight → Build → Test → Listen → Community.
- Reframe the Founding Circle as the transparent conclusion: starting small and shaping conversations, experiments, priorities, and what comes next.
- Add one-time, lightweight scroll reveals and subtle hover/focus states, with immediate content for reduced-motion users.

## Responsive and QA
- Preserve asymmetric composition on desktop, simplify it for tablet, and use a spacious vertical sequence on mobile.
- Verify desktop, tablet, mobile, keyboard focus, reduced motion, links, overflow, layout stability, and browser errors.
